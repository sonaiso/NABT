# Source-bound relational bridge: acceptance fixed before implementation

Extend `relations.analyze` inside the existing external I/O gateway. Reuse
VerifiedReading's original-source and full/clean closure replay and
QualifiedInference's signed requirements; do not introduce another inference
engine or theorem registry. This law checks a supplied, controlled relational
ledger, independently opened target text, exact occurrence, and a selected
reviewed lexical reading. The ledger's truth, free-Arabic syntactic acquisition,
causal discovery and empirical measurement remain separate obligations.

The installed reading domain is اصفرّ/مُصْفَرّ/كَبِرَ/كَبُرَ with finite explicit
spellings. Unmarked كبر retains age and magnitude alternatives. A selected
reading requires a source claim binding that occurrence to that reading; it
does not establish unique intended meaning across unrestricted Arabic.

The immutable requirement profiles are cumulative: disposition; occurrence;
causality. All require exact membership, reading, event meaning, bearer,
property, capability, conditions, explicit blocker absence, place and time.
The final review strengthens this profile with the exact full-target and
position keys, occurrence-to-event link, kind/property acceptance and
sense/property link. A same-sense event cannot borrow another occurrence's
evidence; this is an additional rejecting guard, not a relaxation of acceptance.
Occurrence additionally requires occurrence evidence. Causality additionally
requires the exact cause/effect evidence. Caller plans cannot omit these slots.

Acceptance: native source and occurrence replay; mismatch of sense, participant,
event, time or place blocks; no occurrence from capability; no cause from
adjacency/succession; removing the causal witness preserves spatial/temporal
facts and invalidates causal licensing; unrelated alternative support survives;
unknown blocker differs from negative blocker; conflict and tainted derivation
never license; stale/forged source, target, span and receipt rejected; repeated
tokens keep separate occurrences; partial-token selection rejected; missing
native checker fails closed; gateway independently replays the complete result.

Lean must prove the checker condition, original-source binding, source-relative
derivability of every accepted obligation, explicit negative blocker, absence
of promotion without occurrence or causal evidence, conflict blocking, and
rejection of an old certificate after source withdrawal. Examples are authored
and declared. Tests and formal results must report their actual scope.
