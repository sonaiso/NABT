## Purpose and behavior

NABT now connects the rational-inquiry contract to selected classical Arabic
source witnesses. The inquiry contract records the claim, its formation
material and prior information, source lineage, scope, alternatives, context,
conditions and remaining obligations. Its verdict comes from executed checks
and can be replayed from expected inputs supplied independently of the report.

The standalone classical-evidence tool reopens 13 passages from four sources:
Sibawayhi's Kitab, Ibn Hisham's Matn Qatr al-Nada, Jurjani's al-Awamil al-Mia,
and Ibn Faris's Maqayis. It records the exact edition or declared transcription,
source bytes, span, evidence kind, conditional law and remaining application
obligations. This is bounded linguistic evidence, not complete-book conversion.

The added Lean laws preserve the eight grammatical courses and four surface
forms, the full-form inna family with ma-kaffa and both layta alternatives,
and the 91/7/2 Awamil partition. Lexical checks bind the selected gloss to its
registered root entry. A source quotation or conditional calculation does not
acquire the reading of a new target sentence.

The [Arabic guide](../CLASSICAL_LANGUAGE_EVIDENCE.md) and
[source audit](../../evidence/classical_language/SOURCE_AUDIT.md) explain the
book editions, transmission distinctions, counterexamples and scope in detail.

No external event observer or general source-acquisition authentication was added.

## Publication and seal procedure

The user has authorized publishing, committing, approval and sealing. The
second authorized write attempt is recorded in
[publication_attempt_v2.json](../../evidence/classical_language/publication_attempt_v2.json).
GitHub's integration still returned `403 Resource not accessible by integration`.
No secret value is recorded in the repository or evidence.

Once a repository-scoped write credential is available:

1. Push the review branch without force and open the prepared pull request
   against the current `main`; preserve the feature commit in its history.
2. Require successful `formal`, `runtime (3.11)`, `runtime (3.12)` and
   `runtime (3.13)` Actions/check-runs on the exact final head. Inspect actual
   reviews and changes; do not create a self-approval or bypass a required gate.
3. Refresh the base and head, then merge with an expected-head SHA. Use a merge
   commit if the repository permits it, preserving the feature history.
4. Verify the merged commit and its own Actions run before sealing it.
5. Create a new `v0.15.0a1` tag on that exact merge commit and publish it as a
   prerelease with the validation links and retained coverage limits. Do not
   move an existing tag. Record and read back the tag target and release URL.

An annotated tag and the evidence hashes identify the reviewed content. They
must not be described as a cryptographic signature or an immutable server
policy without separately established support for those claims.
