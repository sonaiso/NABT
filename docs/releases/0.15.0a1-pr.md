# Proposed pull request

Title: Bind classical Arabic witnesses to scoped Lean laws and rational inquiry

## Why

A successful formal derivation does not by itself establish that the source
was read correctly or that its rule applies to a new sentence. NABT needs
reviewable evidence linking a precise claim to its source, interpretation,
conditions, counterexamples and scope.

## What changes

- Makes the rational method the explicit organizing principle in the README;
  procedures follow the kind of claim, and Lean checks declared obligations.
  Keep formation, evidence, correspondence and application distinct.
- Integrates the previously prepared rational-inquiry contract with merged PR
  #15 while preserving the registered 75-file runtime snapshot.
- Adds a standalone classical-source tool, a pinned catalog of 13 passages
  from four books, exact report replay, source metadata and ten runnable examples.
- Adds 48 Lean theorems covering source/interpretation/target obligations and
  limited grammatical laws: Sibawayhi's eight courses/four forms, inna/ma/layta
  conditions, and Jurjani's 91/7/2 partition.
- Preserves unknown conditions and competing readings; no caller-supplied
  interpretation becomes an acquired target analysis or external-event proof.
- Retains the source-classification correction, budget defect and repair,
  historical freeze failure and exact registered-freeze test correction.
- Documents data attribution, source scope and the prepared alpha release.

## Scope and retained limitations

Construction remains 71/86 and discourse 56/86 with `gate_pass=true` and
`strict_pass=false`. The addition does not formalize all four books or map
Jurjani's complete hundred-item inventory. The Awamil excerpt has a declared
manual transcription. Target readings remain caller-interpreted, and the
source reader/Python implementation are not generally proved refinements of
Lean. No independent external observer or acquisition-authentication authority
is claimed. Development review was not a blind external language evaluation.
