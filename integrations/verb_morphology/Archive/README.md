The Lean files in this directory are verb-related excerpts of empirical studies from
`Saleh1967/Alghanem` at `9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd`, under
`suspended/samai/lean`. The archive verifies counts and counterexamples on the
tables embedded in each file. A matching row has that table's evidential scope.
Plural-specific tables and unrelated census rows were omitted. Closed finite counts are checked with Lean 4.34.1 kernel reduction. Each file retains the author's descriptions of the data and its provenance.

The LexSamai tables were derived from the Arramooz dictionary of Taha Zerrouki
and Mohamed Kebdani, distributed under the GNU General Public License v2.
The Arramooz terms and attribution accompany its derived data, including the
form_II portion of the exported masdar studies. The A116 source license is
preserved in `vendor/alghanem-a116/LICENSE`.
