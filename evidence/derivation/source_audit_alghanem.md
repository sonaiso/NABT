# Alghanem derivation and pattern audit

## Finding and source pin

Alghanem supplies useful contracts for **applying a known template**, **checking candidate root/template pairs**, and **preserving information through declared transformations**. It also contains explicit counterexamples to recovering one root or one grammatical category from shape alone. These support a bounded next step for NABT: retain the source occurrence and all exact structural candidates, then require independent evidence before selecting a lexical or semantic reading.

The audited commit is [`9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/tree/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd). ## Contracts to transfer first

### 1. Typed template application with an exact inverse relation

[`Ishtiqaq.WF`, `extract_fill_wf`, `fill_injective`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Ishtiqaq.lean#L46) provide a general list contract. The template has exactly the three slots, once each and in order; filling that template with a root then extracting at the same positions returns that root. Its **31** declared templates divide into **11 past, 9 passive past and 11 imperative** forms. These facts are complete for that explicit inventory.

The inverse must check more than the existing `extract`: it skips literal positions without comparing their values and returns partial output on length mismatches. A NABT recognizer should validate the root alphabet and arity, preserve the template ID, and require complete forward replay against the observed form. [`Wazn.fillC_determines_root`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Wazn.lean#L92) determines only radicals whose slots occur; `realize_determines_root` additionally assumes the same wasl-vowel value. These are conditional reconstruction properties.

[`QiraaMadlul.sighaCands_iff`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/QiraaMadlul.lean#L94) is the closest complete candidate contract: membership is equivalent to an allowed template and supplied root matching the word. `sigha_wad` prevents a selected root from appearing outside that supplied list. Its example inventory contains **eight templates**, **four roots**, and **three root-to-masdar rows**. Its candidate output discards template identity into `(root, Naw)`, and its noun match relation deliberately ignores the final vowel. NABT should preserve both the template identity and explicit matching mode instead. `Wad.ofStr3` is a helper taking three characters with defaults, so arbitrary caller strings need a stronger typed boundary.

### 2. Keep ambiguity as a result

[`Ishtiqaq.form_alone_does_not_determine_root`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Ishtiqaq.lean#L216) constructs one surface from VII(ت ب ر) and VIII(ن ب ر). [`QismAwamil.amr_faail_yashtabih`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/QismAwamil.lean#L154) shows the faail predicate accepts قَاتِلْ, جَاهِدِ and كَاتِبُ. The earlier comparative/imperfect witness makes the same point on another template.

Weak-root transformations add information loss: [`Neutral.neutral_forgets_weak`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Neutral.lean#L171) maps both قول and قيل to قال; `neutral_identifies_lit_and_radical` collapses two different ءمن template analyses. [`Wad.deposit_not_function`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Wad.lean#L154) states the analogous non-function boundary for the declared lexical relation. No first-match or default classification may erase these alternatives.

### 3. Reversible transformation receipts

[`Recovery.edit_roundtrip`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Recovery.lean#L25) is a general list-edit restoration theorem. `restoration_forces_fiber_separation` makes the residual-information requirement explicit: if different inputs collapse to one output, exact recovery needs information that distinguishes them. This fits NABT's existing source/occurrence/reading-bound receipts. Restoration proves data preservation; linguistic authorization for the edit is a separate requirement.

### 4. Delay broad conjugation transfer until its conditions are explicit

`Tasrif` uses **250** abstract feature states and lifts a finite cell-law check to every supplied triliteral `NoAlif` root. `madi_exhausted`, `mudari_exhausted`, and `amr_exhausted` prove properties of those declared rules. Its imperfect/imperative scope has two babs, four prefixes, three supplied middle vowels and three ending choices. The header explicitly says that choosing the six lexical babs is external: phonological licensing permits all nine past/imperfect vowel combinations in its model.

`Mudari.reference_licensed` instead quantifies over **11 reference roots**, nine forms and the declared `babs` list. Its general `amr_head_vowelled` theorem proves the output's initial-vowel property. `amr_restores_mudari` is a finite reference-list calculation that excludes named roots with deleted hamza. These scopes must remain separate. `Rubai.rubai_licensed` lifts **625** root-class states across its six entries; this still proves cell-law licensing, not every generated item's lexical reality.

## Exceptions and misleading transfers

- **Wasl with root lam:** [`Wazn.wasl_lam_conflict`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Wazn.lean#L358) records a conflict between the article-oriented rule and the verb rule. [`Mudari.amr_lqy`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Mudari.lean#L241) uses the latter. Do not copy the broad entry-vowel function into a verb generator unchanged.
- **Hamza module fork:** [`Tasrif.hamza_fork`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Tasrif.lean#L799) gives different outputs from `Mudari`; `agrees_with_mudari` excludes first/middle-hamza roots and has a restricted reference domain. There is no universal equivalence theorem between the two implementations.
- **Named irregulars:** `Mudari.takhfifRoots` declares رأى; `hadhfFaRoots` declares أخذ/أكل/أمر. `Tasrif.amrKhudh` and `amrPause` contain separate deposited behavior. A syllable-law pass cannot supply the missing lexical premise.
- **Unavailable quadriliteral:** [`Rubai.ifanlala_refused`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Rubai.lean#L68) deliberately leaves افعنلل unavailable because its profile lacks a sealed vowel witness. It is a named coverage boundary, not a proof that the Arabic form is impossible.
- **Degree is supplied:** `RootGrade.table_length`, `num_inj`, and `num_unnum` are exhaustive enumeration/codec contracts for `28^n` strings. They do not discover original radicals or prove that every string is a root. `khumasi_jamid` follows the declared degree classification.
- **Derivation graph:** [`Derivation.edges` and `stationary_unique`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Derivation.lean#L31) concern a fixed ten-node, nine-edge Markov graph. The stationary arithmetic is not a lexical derivation algorithm, pattern recognizer or empirical distribution of Arabic.
- **Pattern versus meaning:** [`Dalalat.wazn_la_yuayyin_mutawaa`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Dalalat.lean#L203) directly refutes a pattern-only mutawaa decision on its deposited examples. The semantic jamid/mushtaq axis, original-radical degree and verb conjugation class remain distinct.
- **Future:** [`Istiqama.sawfa_ams`, `mudari_la_muhal`](https://github.com/Saleh1967/Alghanem/blob/9d8fa03a51f894416e0abaf3b6821c5ae6441dcd/formal/a116/A116/Istiqama.lean#L206) are useful conditional temporal relations once the verb and particle features are witnessed. `QismKhanat.badLam` uses adjacency as a rule in that upstream profile; it must not be imported to manufacture missing lexical kind in NABT.

## Recommended NABT boundary

Implement a typed finite template inventory and exact structural candidate relation first, retaining the raw source, the root proposal, the template, normalization choices, repeated slots and all alternatives. A candidate can be proposed from a new string without a supplied answer, but a matched template label remains a structural reading. Root attestation, noun subtype, voice or tense in use, lexical bab, and actual syntactic role require their own evidence.

Expose all requested category names with honest implementation status. Where this source supplies no operational class contract, preserve a named unresolved boundary and add source-backed contracts later. Reprove transferred algebra against NABT's own representation and sound law; this review does not equate Alghanem's `strictB` with NABT validity.

The quoted declaration excerpts in the companion JSON retain the upstream MIT notice and exact immutable locations. The primary grammar passages cited by upstream comments were not reverified against whole books in this code audit.
