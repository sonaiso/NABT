# Separate source-premise counterexamples

These six directed tests were authored before inspecting or executing the new hasiba guard. They reuse an explicitly authored teaching fixture, not classical quotations as training records. The primary source passage supplies the narrow meaning/form rule, while raw QAC-format rows are declared local premises. The two levels of authority are separate.

Two controls supply a fully marked active perfect 3MS form-I `Hasiba`; four counterexamples supply a contradictory form-II marker, unmarked source form, contrary medial vowel, or passive source. Every target sentence remains identical. The target cannot complete or replace source observations. Negative outcomes concern source-relative licensing, not the intrinsic grammaticality of the target.

These cases do not change any frozen case or denominator. Their independent result is reported alongside the existing 24 cases / 29 questions. A passing negative needs actual public reading and replay, independent raw receipt validation, no licensed cognitive belief, and no projection of embedded fact. A missing report or thrown exception is an error, never a successful refusal. Positive controls also require the belief question to be supported and the embedded factual question to remain unknown.

The same original source passage is pinned in `cases.json`; the authored raw profiles are pinned below. No runtime fix output was used to set these expected outcomes. This is a review-motivated counterexample suite, not a new blind or statistical experiment.
