The diagnostic matrix references the existing, unchanged catalogue at
`evidence/lexical_completion_v1/catalog.json`. Its OpenITI-derived dictionary
quotations and annotations remain under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, attributed to Ibn
Manzur, Ibn Faris, OpenITI / KITAB and the source digitization contributors.
See `../lexical_denotation/NOTICE.md` and the immutable source coordinates in
that catalogue. The Shakhsiyya excerpt retains its existing supplied-source
terms and is a logical example, not a dictionary source.

Quranic Arabic Corpus morphology v0.4 is attributed to Kais Dukes (2011),
<http://corpus.quran.com>. The original source includes a GPL label and a
verbatim-copy/no-change notice, and Tanzil text attribution with CC BY-ND 3.0
terms. Its complete notice block remains inside the original, unmodified
external file. No corpus file, edited corpus, or new synthetic QAC dataset is
redistributed by this directory. Exact source offsets, annotation identities,
and derived diagnostic metadata support replay against that external input.
The original full text is not an installed runtime dependency of NABT.

Dictionary and QAC source agreement is not independent transmission or a
judgment that the QAC occurrence bears a selected dictionary sense. The
matrix may retain several consonantally compatible but differently vocalized
lemmas and senses. No number, gender, grammatical case, denotation, physical
fact, or motive is supplied by default to fill missing evidence.
