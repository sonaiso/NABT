# Evaluation data and source attribution

The sixty controlled-Arabic episodes in `controlled_text_benchmark.json` and
their manual gold answers were newly authored for this experiment. They are
distributed under the repository's MIT license. They are synthetic evaluation
fixtures, not quotations from grammatical books and not independently sampled
natural Arabic.

archives retain **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0
