# Retained source evidence

The Thinking file identifies its author as Taqi al-Din al-Nabhani; the supplied
first volume of *al-Shakhsiyya al-Islamiyya* also names him. The supplied third
volume is cataloged with the same work as provided. These identifications are
source metadata, not independent authentication of authorship or a printed edition.

The retained excerpts are the selected paragraphs used for the requested
formalization and audit. Original DOC/DOCX files are not distributed here.
Their textual content is attributed to the supplied works and is not asserted
to be MIT-licensed software. Repository code and authored interpretations are
separate from quoted source text. Exact selection, extraction method and
artifact hashes are in `sources.json` and `original_comparison.json`.

The Awamil inventory transcribes only numbered entries of the historical body
in the pinned scan. It excludes modern editorial notes. The existing
`evidence/classical_language/sources/awamil_jurjani_excerpt.metadata.json`
identifies the scan and edition. Page labels here are PDF page numbers.
Transcription removed vocalization and added functional labels where necessary
to distinguish repeated written forms. See `awamil_inventory.json` for variants.

Native byte checks establish textual occurrence and source identity. They do
not establish the correctness of an interpretation, independence of witnesses,
authenticity of a narration, or truth in the world.
