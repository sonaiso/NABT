Each excerpt retains its exact pinned source URL, whole-file hash, byte interval,
physical line and preceding page marker in `real_excerpts.json`. Selection and
annotation were added for this experiment. No byte inside a selected interval
was normalized, vocalized, corrected or silently cleaned. Separate surrounding
contexts are supplied to make the selection inspectable. No endorsement by the
authors, editors or corpus providers is claimed.

a separate license audit of every printed edition or editorial contribution.
